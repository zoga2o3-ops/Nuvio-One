# Nuvio One 1.1.0-beta.13.5

## Music

- Autogenerated mixes and personal playlists now open as full queues instead of playing one preview track. Next and Previous move through the mix.
- The music video player now has Previous, Next, Shuffle and Repeat, an Up next queue, and a Switch to audio button that resumes the song at the same moment.
- The Now Playing album name is a link. Album pages use YouTube Music's own title, artist and cover instead of the card that opened them.
- Artist pages show a Popular section, the artist description and their release shelves, even when the artist has no top songs.
- Recently played now keeps up to 500 tracks and shows the whole list, not just the latest 30.
- Fixed Music sign-in persistence: refreshed tokens are saved, and a stale Music session is restored instead of hiding your playlists.

## Home

- Home rows now populate on their own once their catalog loads, without needing to move focus. Previously a completed catalog could stay as gray loading cards until a row was highlighted.
- A catalog addon that stops responding no longer holds up the rest of Home. Its row is removed after a timeout instead of staying as gray loading cards.

## Live TV

- Large M3U playlists parse about 19x faster: a synthetic 100,000-channel list went from 2.08 s to 0.11 s. Movie and episode rows are skipped before any allocation.
- The player's Backups button appears as soon as the channel index is ready, without reopening the player.
- Fixed blank addon posters on Fire TV: a custom WebP decoder used for a Fire OS crash fix was intercepting every static WebP poster. Coil's normal decoder is used again.
- Google Drive backup now uses Nuvio One's own Google sign-in. If you use it, reconnect Drive once after updating; your TV's setup is untouched.
- The Drive sign-in QR now opens Google's page with the code already filled in, so pairing no longer needs typing the code.
- Cloudflare setup sync now follows the same first-sync rules as Google Drive: favorites, hidden channels and groups, and your group order are kept instead of being replaced by the saved copy.
- Home no longer reads the whole TV guide at startup: the full guide is read when you open Live TV. With a very large guide this keeps posters and navigation responsive instead of waiting behind a full guide scan.
- Sports now uses the saved guide sports index, so matches no longer rescan every channel's listings on each update.
- Playlist order is now preserved when two TVs' setups are combined, so reordering playlists syncs to your other TVs.
- Group order is kept on the first setup sync too, so a group reorder is not dropped when the other TV has never saved one.
- Continue Watching "X EP left" now counts only episodes that have aired.

## Player

- New **Playback Info** setting (off by default) shows resolution, codec and playback speed above the title.
- New **Visible Buttons** setting chooses which player controls appear. The Info button is hidden by default and Play always stays visible.

## Navigation

- On any section other than Home, the first Back now opens the Nuvio side menu. The next Back shows “Press back again to exit”, and the following Back closes the app. Home keeps its existing exit prompt.

## Sports

- Sports now reads ESPN's public feed directly for scores, schedules, teams and logos. `sporting-events.org` still supplies date-only events. TV listings, player rosters and some TheSportsDB-only schedules are temporarily unavailable while a new data service is prepared.

## On Demand

- Returning from a movie or series no longer rebuilds the On Demand rows from the first page. Your scroll position and focused title are kept, and the page only reloads when the catalog changed.
