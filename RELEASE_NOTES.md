# Nuvio One 1.1.0-beta.13.5

Music queues, a faster Live TV guide, more sports leagues, player button controls, and new navigation.

## Music

- Autogenerated mixes and personal playlists now open as full queues. Next and Previous move through the mix.
- The music video player now has Previous, Next, Shuffle and Repeat, an Up next queue, and a Switch to audio button that resumes the song at the same moment.
- The Now Playing album name is a link. Album pages use YouTube Music's own title, artist and cover.
- Artist pages show a Popular section, the artist description and their release shelves, even when the artist has no top songs.
- Recently played now keeps up to 500 tracks and shows the whole list.

## Home and Continue Watching

- Continue Watching's “X EP left” badge now appears immediately and updates as the show's episode list loads.
- “X EP left” now counts only episodes that have aired.

## Live TV

- Large M3U playlists parse about 19x faster: a synthetic 100,000-channel list went from 2.08 s to 0.11 s.
- The player's Backups button appears as soon as the channel index is ready.
- Fixed blank addon posters on Fire TV.

## Player

- New **Playback Info** setting (off by default) shows resolution, codec and playback speed above the title.
- New **Visible Buttons** setting chooses which player controls appear. The Info button is hidden by default and Play always stays visible.

## Navigation

- On any section other than Home, the first Back now opens the Nuvio side menu. The next Back shows “Press back again to exit”, and the following Back closes the app. Home keeps its existing exit prompt.

## Sports

- Sports now includes 97 extra football leagues with tables and team badges.
- Added WWE, AEW, TNA and boxing schedules.
