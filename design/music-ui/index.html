Four setups. One listening room.

The prototype ships with four colour directions. Aurora is the chosen one: a deep ink canvas, album-derived violet/cyan light, and a coral edge for energy. Ember is the warm vinyl room; Pulse is the late-night club; Daylight is the editorial daytime mode for tablet and phone.

What the best apps get right

  • Apple Music: full-bleed art, colour-extracted backgrounds, lyrics as a primary surface, immersive now playing.
  • Spotify TV: square album art, familiar shelves, a persistent queue and easy account switching.
  • YouTube Music: split now playing with up-next visible; criticised when action chips get cramped and the library feels secondary.
  • Plexamp / Sonos / Tidal: persistent player, content-first home, universal search, UltraBlur-style colour, immersive artwork.

Nuvio One's point of view

Library-first, lyric-first, living-room-first. Not another phone layout stretched to 16:9. The remote is the cursor: every action is a large focus target, the queue/lyrics live in a visible Sidecar, and the Canvas gives lyrics the whole screen when the room wants to sing.

Focus & motion

  • Focus = 2px light ring + soft halo + 2–4px lift. No tiny targets.
  • The vinyl turns only while playing; the aurora background breathes with the track, not a looping video.
  • Back always restores the previous focus. Panels never steal the remote.
  • Every list has a clear current row and a visible next action.

TV / tablet / phone

  • TV: left rail, hero, horizontal shelves, bottom mini player, full-screen now playing.
  • Tablet: collapsed icon rail, two-column hero, same now playing; more content per row.
  • Phone: bottom nav, stacked hero, 2-up cards, mini player above the nav, now playing as a sheet.

The four setups

Aurora · chosen Ember · warm vinyl Pulse · club Daylight · editorial

Why Aurora: it is distinctive without becoming a gimmick. The violet/cyan light reads as "music" without copying Spotify green or YouTube red, the coral edge adds warmth, and the ink base keeps album art and lyrics as the brightest things on screen. Ember is the alternate if the room is more vinyl than neon; Pulse is for party mode; Daylight is the daytime/tablet mode.

Try it

Switch TV / Tablet / Phone above. Use the remote or arrow keys to move focus, OK/Enter to select, Back/Esc to close Now Playing. Click any lyric line to seek. Switch themes with the four dots. Open Canvas in Now Playing for the full-screen lyric mode.
