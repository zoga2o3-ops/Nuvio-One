# Nuvio One Music

**Now available in Nuvio One.**

Your TV is now a music player. Here's what you can do:

- **YouTube Music** - sign in for your playlists, liked songs and personalised home. Likes sync across your devices.
- **Spotify playlists** - import any public playlist by pasting its link.
- **Navidrome and WebDAV** - connect your own music server and stream your collection.
- **Local music** - play music stored on your TV, USB drive or network share.
- **Move your music** - send downloaded songs to your PC from the Local page, or back them up to Google Drive.
- **Offline listening** - download tracks and play them without a connection.
- **Lyrics** - synced lyrics appear automatically while you listen.
- **Music videos** - watch full screen, then jump straight back to the song.
- **Build your collection** - create playlists, edit the queue, like songs, and start radio or shuffle.
- **Explore and search** - browse moods and genres, or find any track.
- **Last.fm** - scrobble what you listen to.
- **Party mode** - let guests add songs to the queue.
- **Made for the remote** - simple top tabs, Up Next and easy Back navigation.
